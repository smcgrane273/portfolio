# Tech A

## In Class Project

**Role / note:** This semester in Technology A was my first experience learning to

code, and it opened up an entirely new way of thinking about systems,

storytelling, design, and data. At its core, this class became, for me,

about exploring the connective tissue of the world around us, the

intangible systems and rhythms that quietly shape daily life made

visible through data, interaction, and the technologies at our fingertips.

My first project transformed the manual for a tide clock I had repaired

into a website, balancing the tiny mechanical details of coding and

clock systems with the larger celestial rhythms that govern the device.

(A,B,C) From there, continuing to explore the unseen systems that

structure daily life, I created an interactive world map visualizing New

York City’s food imports. This project allowed users to directly engage

with information about how everyday food items arrive where they do,

including their global journeys and associated costs. (D)

For my final project, Drawing Time,(E) I built an interactive camera

using physical computing to record and composite data over a set

period, creating a portrait of a specific space and moment in time.

Across these projects, I found a clear throughline: mapping and

measurement, understanding how things arrive where they are, and

moving between the enormous and the minute to build a thoughtful

design practice rooted in close attention and curiosity. Building on the

themes of the course, this work reflects an ongoing exploration of the

balance between technological interaction and the power of data.
