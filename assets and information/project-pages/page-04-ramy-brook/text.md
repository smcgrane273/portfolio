Graphic Design

Format: Graphic Design

I supported graphic design and photo editing needs, helping shape seasonal campaigns that stayed true to the brand's color palette, typography, and overall aesthetic. My goal was always to make the layouts visually clear and engaging while enhancing the stories each campaign aimed to tell.
