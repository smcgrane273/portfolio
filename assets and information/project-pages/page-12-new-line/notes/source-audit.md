## Original Site Behavior

- Loads p5.js from a CDN.
- Creates a full-window canvas with a warm beige background.
- Stores multiple simple object outlines as normalized point arrays.
- Resamples each outline into 420 points.
- Morphs continuously between current and target shapes.
- Adds subtle noise drift and variable stroke weight.
- Shows a small recording dot in the upper-right corner.
- Double-clicking the record dot captures a 12-second `.webm` recording.
