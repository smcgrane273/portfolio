# ONE Pilates

## Detail Studio

**Role / note:** Through my work with Detail Studio, I had the opportunity to help

develop visual assets for ONE Pilates, a new studio focused on move-

ment and flow. This project was especially meaningful to me because

it marked my first sustained experience working with typography.

What initially felt unfamiliar quickly became confidence-building

and creatively expansive. I worked on imagery and graphics for so-

cial media, shaping a visual language drawn from the studio’s sym-

bols and philosophy. By exploring type as form and integrating it

seamlessly into images, I developed an aesthetic grounded in clarity,

movement, and calm.
