# Dark Room Trash

## A Study in Discard

**Role / note:** This book is a curation of the contents of a darkroom trash can. Neither

found photography nor intentional creation, the images exist somewhere

in between. Many of these photographs never saw the light. Their

potential and full chemical development were only realized through my

exploration of the material as I collected and assembled them into a book,

exposing them to light in the process. The book was exhibited at the

MoMA PS1 Art Book Fair in 2025.
