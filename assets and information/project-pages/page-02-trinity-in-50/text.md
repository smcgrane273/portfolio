# Trinity in 50

## Detail Studio

**Role / note:** Trinity in 50 tells the story of Trinity College Dublin through fifty

objects chosen by students, staff, and alumni that reflect the

institution’s storied past. I assisted Al Higgins in photographing the

selected items, including the historic Blue Clock above the Front

Gate, once synchronized to Dublin Mean Time, and the cobblestone

path, partially paved over nearly 100 years ago, that leads students

through the institution. Each image carries its own story and

interpretation of Trinity’s past, and the book reflects the many

perspectives that have passed through the Front Gate since the

university’s founding in 1592. Images and text were carefully aligned

so that each object had its own space to speak clearly, collectively

building a portrait of the institution and its layered history.

The project’s careful approach to storytelling and design was

recognized with an ICAD award.
