# Content Notes

## Working Title

Pointillist Eyes

## Alternate Portfolio Title

Blink

## Suggested Subtitle

A pointillist browser portrait with a blink interaction.

## Short Description

A p5.js sketch that translates a photograph of eyes into a field of colored dots, then briefly swaps to a closed-eye image when clicked.

## Longer Draft Description

`Pointillist Eyes` uses image sampling to reconstruct a cropped eye portrait as a field of dots. Darker sampled pixels become larger marks, while lighter areas recede into the background. The interaction is minimal: a click triggers a short blink by swapping the open-eye dot field with a closed-eye version.
