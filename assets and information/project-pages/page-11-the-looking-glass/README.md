## Project Summary

`The Looking Glass` is an interactive fogged mirror. The page requests access to the user-facing camera, mirrors and softens the video feed, then covers it with a layer of condensation. Dragging across the screen wipes the fog away, creating temporary openings through the glass.

The project exists in two source forms:

- `index.html`: self-contained HTML/CSS/JS version with inline styles and camera/canvas logic.
- `p5-sketch.js`: p5.js sketch version of the same fogged-mirror interaction.

## Strong Visual Moments

- The page behaves like a fogged mirror rather than a conventional website.
- The user’s camera feed becomes blurred, darkened, and reversed.
- The condensation layer turns the screen into a surface that can be touched.
- Wiping gestures create soft openings through the fog.
- The piece balances intimacy, reflection, obstruction, and reveal.
