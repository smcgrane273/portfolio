## Original Site Behavior

- Requests the user-facing camera through `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia`.
- Mirrors the camera feed horizontally.
- Applies blur, saturation, contrast, brightness, mist, glow, and glass effects.
- Paints a fog layer on a canvas.
- Pointer/touch dragging erases fog from the canvas.
- Window resize repaints the fog and atmospheric layers.
