# Content Notes

## Working Title

The Looking Glass

## Suggested Subtitle

An interactive fogged mirror for the browser.

## Short Description

An intimate browser-based mirror that turns the user-facing camera into a fogged glass surface. Touching or dragging across the screen wipes away condensation and briefly reveals the image underneath.

## Longer Draft Description

The piece uses the browser camera as a live reflective material. A blurred, mirrored video feed sits beneath layers of mist, glow, condensation, droplets, and glass. The viewer interacts by dragging across the surface, erasing the fog in soft circular gestures. The result feels somewhere between a mirror, a window, and a screen that remembers touch.
