# Content Notes

## Working Title

The Highway Book

## Alternate Portfolio Title

Best Overpasses America

## Suggested Subtitle

Ten American highway interchanges arranged as an editorial image book.

## Short Description

A digital and printable image book studying large-scale American overpasses and interchanges through a restrained sequence of aerial infrastructure images, captions, counters, and source metadata.

## Longer Draft Description

The project gathers ten major American highway interchanges and presents them with the pacing of an image book. Each interchange appears as a single plate with a quiet caption line describing place, highways, and structural type. The interface uses simple previous/next navigation, keyboard movement, a counter, and a source panel, while the companion booklet turns the same material into a print-oriented sequence.
