# Mad River

A Film

Format: Film
Focus: Art Direction / Content Manager

Mad River was an incredible project to be part of. I was lucky to serve as the art director for my friend Rowan's film, an ode to his family's land in Northern California. Inspired by the landscape and the film's quietly powerful narrative, I developed a visual language and identity that guided the project from its earliest stages through fundraising and outreach, festival submissions, and its life in the public eye.

In many ways, the landscape in Mad River is a character in itself, whose stories, presence, and daily rhythms shape the film.

We made Mad River on the ranch where Rowan Gould-Bayba grew up. Davis Gould-Baybas, producer/cinematographer, and Kahlil Gould-Baybas, actor, photographed his raised cattle for more than 25 years.

We couldn't have made this film without the support, creativity, generosity, and kindness of Rowan and Kahlil's family, the community in Petrolia, CA, and our brilliant cast and crew.
